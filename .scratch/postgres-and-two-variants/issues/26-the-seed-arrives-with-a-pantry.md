# 26 - The Seed arrives with a Pantry

Status: done

**What to build:** The Seed ticks a handful of Pantry Ingredients as part of the fixture, so a
restored Demo Variant answers `GET /api/state` with a populated Best Matches instead of an empty one.

**Why this is not a recording problem.** Ticket 21 captured the Seed into the bundle and found the
ranking empty. The reason first written down was wrong and is worth correcting here too: the ranking
does not need a Pantry tick. `bestMatches.js` admits a Recipe on either of two grounds, Pantry
overlap or nothing Missing, and the second exists for the Recipe whose Ingredients are all Staples.
The Seed satisfies neither. No Seed Recipe is built from Staples alone, and a restored database has
no ticks.

So the empty ranking is not an artefact of the capture. It is what the live Demo Variant serves too,
to every Reviewer, until they tick something themselves. Fixing it in the recorder would have fixed
the smaller half of the problem and made the recording a staged scenario rather than the state a
restore produces, which is the equivalence that lets the suite check the committed file at all
([ADR-0009](../../../docs/adr/0009-degraded-mode-from-a-recorded-seed.md)).

**What is being lost meanwhile.** `seedFixture.js` says in its own header that the collection was
built so that "ticking a handful of Pantry Ingredients ranks nine of the nineteen at three different
depths of Missing, instead of matching one Recipe and calling that a demonstration". That design is
currently unreachable without the visitor doing the ticking, and during an outage it is unreachable
at all, because the tick is a write degraded mode disables.

**The shape of the change.** The Seed already marks Staples and shelves Aisles through the API's own
routes ([ADR-0007](../../../docs/adr/0007-seed-loads-through-the-api.md)); ticks go the same way, and
`restoreSeed` gains no second path into the database. Choosing which Ingredients to tick is the real
work: enough to rank Recipes at more than one depth of Missing, few enough that the Shopping List
still has something to buy, and none of them Staples, which never enter the Pantry.

**What to watch.** The recorded Seed is regenerated by the build and compared byte for byte, so this
ticket's change turns `packages/api/test/recorded-seed.test.js` red until the recording is
regenerated. That is the mechanism working. Run `npm run record`.

**Blocked by:** None. 12 supplied the fixture, 21 supplied the recording and the staleness check.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** The Seed fixture declares Pantry ticks and the restore loads them through the API, so a restored Demo Variant serves a populated Best Matches.

**Current behavior:**
The Seed fixture declares Recipes, Staples, Aisles and Selected Recipes. The restore loads each
through the API's own routes and nothing else. A freshly restored database therefore has no Pantry
ticks, and since no Seed Recipe is built from Staples alone, `GET /api/state` answers with an empty
Best Matches — live after every restore, and permanently in the recorded fallback degraded mode
serves during an outage.

**Desired behavior:**
The fixture names a set of Ingredients as Pantry ticks, and the restore applies them through the
existing per-Ingredient Pantry route, the same way Staples and Aisles load. After a restore,
`GET /api/state` ranks Recipes at more than one depth of Missing. The fixture's own header states
the design target: a handful of ticks ranking nine of the nineteen Recipes at three different
depths of Missing. The Shopping List must still have entries to buy after the ticks.

**Key interfaces:**
- The Seed fixture gains a collection of Ingredient names to tick. Every name must be an Ingredient
  some seeded Recipe calls for, and none may be a Staple — the Pantry route refuses Staples, and the
  loader's existing pattern is to fail the restore loudly naming the offending entry, which is the
  behavior to keep.
- The restore function loads ticks via the Pantry endpoint with no second path into the database
  (ADR-0007's rule). Ordering: ticks against Staples would be refused, so tick only non-Staples;
  otherwise placement among the existing load steps is the agent's choice.
- The recorded Seed is regenerated with `npm run record`; the byte-for-byte staleness test will be
  red until it is, which is that mechanism working as designed.
- ADR-0009's consequences currently explain why the recorded Best Matches is empty and defer the fix
  to this ticket. Update that account to describe the populated ranking.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] The restore applies the fixture's Pantry ticks through the API route, with no direct database
      write added
- [ ] After a restore, `GET /api/state` returns Best Matches ranking Recipes at three depths of
      Missing, per the fixture header's stated design
- [ ] The Shopping List in the same response is non-empty
- [ ] A fixture tick naming a Staple or an unknown Ingredient fails the restore with an error naming
      the entry
- [ ] The recorded Seed is regenerated and its staleness test passes
- [ ] ADR-0009 no longer describes the recorded Best Matches as empty

**Out of scope:**
- The recorder: no ticking during capture; the recording stays a snapshot of what a restore produces
- The ranking rule itself and its two admission grounds
- The Staples list and which Recipes are Selected
- Degraded mode's read-only behavior
